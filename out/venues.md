# Publication Venues for a Low-Cost Bin-to-Curb Robot (2027 cycle)

**Date researched:** 2026-10-03
**Project:** low-cost autonomous robot that moves unmodified 64/96-gal municipal carts from garage to curb and back. Two high-school authors. Build Dec 2026 to Feb 2027. Target submission Mar 1-2, 2027.

**Method note.** Facts come from official conference pages, the IEEE RAS event calendar, and the PaperPlaza (ras.papercept.net) conference list, all opened with WebFetch on 2026-10-03. The shared WebSearch budget ran out early in this session, so most pages were reached by direct fetch and by following links. Labels used in this file:
- **CONFIRMED**: read on a page linked here.
- **ESTIMATE**: projected from earlier editions.
- **INFERENCE**: my own judgment.
- **UNVERIFIED**: could not be confirmed.

Where two official sources disagree, both are given and the conference's own site is preferred. No acceptance rate could be confirmed for any venue. Statements about "acceptance odds" are inference.

> **Most important finding:** IROS 2027 and CASE 2027 both close on **Mon Mar 1, 2027**. The IROS time is reported as 23:59 America/Los_Angeles, which is Mar 2 07:59 UTC. A submission on "Mar 2" local time would miss both. AIM 2027 moved its paper deadline **earlier, to Jan 26, 2027**. ARSO, SoutheastCon and RoboSoft also close before Mar 1. Treat **Mar 1, 2027 at 23:59 Pacific** as the hard stop.

## 1. IEEE/RSJ IROS 2027

**Dates, location, deadline**
- Sep 26 - Oct 1, 2027, Fortezza da Basso, Florence, Italy. Theme: **"Human-Centric Robots for Sustainable Life"**. The listed topics include robotics for climate change, **recycling** and sustainable manufacturing (CONFIRMED: [2027.ieee-iros.org](https://2027.ieee-iros.org/)). PaperPlaza lists the dates as Sep 25-30 and says "Submission links will be available soon" ([PaperPlaza](https://ras.papercept.net/conferences/scripts/start.pl)).
- **Paper deadline: Mar 1, 2027** (CONFIRMED on the [IEEE RAS deadline listing](https://www.ieee-ras.org/event/call-for-papers-paper-submission-deadline-iros-2027-ieee-rsj-international-conference-on-intelligent-robots-and-systems-iros-27403-0/) and the [RAS IROS 2027 event page](https://www.ieee-ras.org/event/2027-ieee-rsj-international-conference-on-intelligent-robots-and-systems-iros-70525/)). The aggregator [mldeadlines](https://mldeadlines.com/conference/iros/) gives "1 March 2027, 23:59:59 America/Los_Angeles". The official CFP was not posted as of 2026-10-03.
- Earlier deadlines:
  - IROS 2026: **Mar 2, 2026**. "Submissions may be modified, but not started, through March 5". Video due Mar 7. Notifications Jun 16 ([2026.ieee-iros.org](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/)).
  - IROS 2024 and 2023: Mar 1 (per [mldeadlines](https://mldeadlines.com/conference/iros/)).
  - IROS 2025: UNVERIFIED.
- **Mar 1-2 verdict: on time only if submitted by Mon Mar 1, 23:59 PT.** The 2026 edition let authors edit a submission for 3 more days after the deadline, but only if they had started it before. That grace window is not guaranteed for 2027 (INFERENCE).

**Format and logistics (IROS 2026 rules, CONFIRMED; 2027 expected to be similar, ESTIMATE)**
- "Papers should be up to eight pages… includes the references… there is no option to pay for additional pages". Review is double-anonymous. An optional 1-minute video (≤10 MB) can be attached ([IROS 2026 CFP](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/contribute/call-for-papers/)).
- All accepted papers go into IEEE Xplore. Papers "are to be presented, in person" (same CFP).
- Registration in 2026, early-bird rates ([registration](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/attend/registration/)):
  - IEEE member $920; IEEE student member **$460**; non-member $1,150; student non-member $520.
  - "Non-student registrants can upload up to 2 items; other registrations allow 1 item". So a student registration covered one paper.
  - Whether a high-school student qualifies for the student rate is UNVERIFIED. Ask the organizers.
- Acceptance rate: not found. The 2026 program lists "over 1,900 contributed papers" ([program](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/)), but no submission count was published there.
- Awards in 2026 included "Best Application… and Mobile Manipulation Paper Awards" and "Robot Mechanisms and Design" ([awards](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/awards/)).

**Scope fit**
The 2026 CFP topics include "service robots", "system integration", "robot design", "autonomous vehicles (land…)" and "robot perception". The 2027 theme adds sustainability and recycling. A docking-and-towing service robot with a measured cost-performance trade-off fits well.

**Student / pre-college authors**
No authorship restriction appeared on the IROS pages read. No verified IROS paper with a high-school author was found; searching was cut short by the exhausted budget, so that remains an open TODO.

**Example accepted papers in this area**
- *RaGNNarok* (IROS 2025): "8 pages, accepted by IROS 2025". It runs on a "low-cost Raspberry Pi 5" and was tested on real UGV hardware in three indoor environments for localization, SLAM and navigation ([arXiv 2507.00937](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00937)).
- *Fast and Robust Bio-inspired Teach and Repeat Navigation* (IROS 2021). Real-robot study, more than 6,000 m driven on two platforms (MiRo, Jackal), indoors and outdoors. Built for low-resolution cameras and poor odometry ([arXiv 2010.11326](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11326)).
- *Navigation without localisation: reliable teach and repeat based on the convergence theorem* (IROS 2018). A theoretical error model plus indoor and outdoor trials, with open code and datasets ([arXiv 1711.05348](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05348)).
- Pattern (INFERENCE): 6-8 pages, real-hardware trials, comparison against a baseline, a quantitative error or success table, and open-source release.

**Fit: 8/10.** The deadline matches exactly, the 2027 theme fits, 8 pages leave room for a cost-plus-trials paper, and the student registration covered a paper in 2026. Name recognition for college applications is the highest of any venue here. Against that: it is the most competitive venue and requires travel to Italy. It needs a clear contribution (for example, a measured cost-versus-reliability study across real carts and driveways), not just "a robot that takes out the trash".

## 2. IEEE CASE 2027 (Automation Science and Engineering)

**Dates, location, deadline (CONFIRMED)**
- Aug 23-27, 2027, Johannes Kepler University (LIT Factory), Linz, Austria ([2027.ieeecase.org](https://2027.ieeecase.org/)). PaperPlaza says Aug 22-27.
- Key dates ([key dates](https://2027.ieeecase.org/key-dates/)):
  - Special session proposals Jan 15, 2027
  - **Regular and special session full papers Mar 1, 2027**
  - **Industry and work-in-progress papers Apr 1, 2027**
  - Acceptance May 15, 2027
  - Final paper and author registration Jun 15, 2027
- Earlier deadlines: CASE 2026 was Mar 15, 2026, extended from Mar 1 ([RAS event](https://www.ieee-ras.org/event/2026-ieee-22nd-international-conference-on-automation-science-and-engineering-case-69030/), [CFP](https://2026.ieeecase.org/call-for-papers/)). CASE 2025 was "March 15, 2025 (23:59:00 PST)", also extended from Mar 1 ([2025.ieeecase.org](https://2025.ieeecase.org/)). The 2027 date matches the original (pre-extension) deadline of the last two editions; whether it will be extended is UNVERIFIED.
- **Mar 1-2 verdict: on time for Mar 1.** The time zone is not yet posted; assume Pacific. The WIP track (Apr 1) is a later fallback.

**Format and logistics (2026 rules, CONFIRMED)** ([CASE 2026 CFP](https://2026.ieeecase.org/call-for-papers/))
- Regular papers: 6 pages, plus 2 at an extra charge (max 8).
- WIP and industry papers: 4 pages, presentation only, "will NOT appear in IEEE Xplore".
- Review was double-blind in 2026 and single-blind in 2025 ([2025 CFP](https://2025.ieeecase.org/call-for-papers/)).
- Registration in 2026 ([registration](https://2026.ieeecase.org/registration/)):
  - IEEE member $600-850; non-member $950-1,000; student IEEE member $350-450; virtual $50-100 less.
  - Every accepted paper needs a **FULL** registration, and "Student registration will not be allowed to upload a final paper". Budget at least about $600 plus travel to Austria.
- Size: CASE 2025 received 626 submissions, a record 481 of them regular papers ([2025.ieeecase.org](https://2025.ieeecase.org/)). The CASE 2026 program index lists 283 regular and 142 special-session papers ([paper index .xlsx](https://2026.ieeecase.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/805/01_CASE2026_Paper_Index_Look_Up_Paper_ID.xlsx)). Acceptance rate: not published.

**Scope fit**
- The 2025 topics include "Human-centered automation", "Sustainability and green automation" and "Smart building and construction". The conference description also mentions "transportation & logistics, and smart cities" ([2025 CFP](https://2025.ieeecase.org/call-for-papers/)).
- Cost-effectiveness and automation economics suit CASE well (INFERENCE).
- Downside: the 2026 (Shenyang, "Smart Industry") and 2027 (LIT Factory) hosts lean industrial.

**Student / pre-college authors**
The Best Student Paper Award is open regardless of "age", but the first author must be a full-time graduate or undergraduate student ([awards](https://2026.ieeecase.org/awards/)). High-school authors can publish but are probably not eligible for that award (INFERENCE). A "Best Application Paper Award" also exists.

**Example accepted papers (CASE 2026 program index; titles only, full text not opened)** ([index](https://2026.ieeecase.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/805/01_CASE2026_Paper_Index_Look_Up_Paper_ID.xlsx))
- "A Real-Time Affordable Predictive Trajectory Tracking Controller for Differential Drive Robots Via Imitation Learning" (Rajkumar, Lucia; special session).
- "Lang2Lift: A Language-Guided Autonomous Forklift System for Outdoor Industrial Pallet Handling" (Hoang Nguyen et al.; regular).
- "Development of a Low-Cost Intelligent Vision-Based Robotic System for Flexible Assembly in Small and Medium Enterprises" (Gomes et al.; regular).
- Also listed: "A Flexible and Energy Efficient Wheeled-Legged Robot for Performing Delivery Tasks on Challenging Terrains" and "An Enhanced YOLOv8-SGBM Fusion Algorithm for Binocular Vision-Driven Waste Recognition…".
- Pattern (INFERENCE): 6-page system and application papers, with "low-cost" or "affordable" as an accepted framing.

**Fit: 7/10.** The deadline matches, cost-performance framing suits automation reviewers, the conference is smaller than IROS, and the WIP fallback is open until Apr 1. Against that: full registration is required (no student upload), the venue leans industrial and it is in Austria.
