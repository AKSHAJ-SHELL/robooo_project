# M3 — Reviewer 2 (Methodology rigor and feasibility for this team)
Scores: N 5 / F 5.5 / I 5 → overall 5.17

## Novelty (5):
I found no paper that holds kinematics fixed and changes only leader-arm friction and detents, with downstream ACT precision as the outcome. That gap is real but narrow. Three things weaken it. (a) The modality comparisons the proposal cites are real: "How to Train Your Robots?" (arXiv 2503.07017, Li/Cui/Sadigh, which found kinesthetic data gave the cleanest data and the best learning) and "Static Is Not Enough" (2601.13042, HRI'26 LBR). (b) Hardware groups have already treated leader friction as a design variable. ALOHA 2 (2405.02292) changed to lower-friction, lower-gear-ratio leader gripper motors and added gravity compensation. LeRobot itself went from the SO-100 leader (gears pulled out of the STS3215s, so encoder only and low friction) to the SO-101 leader (geared 1/191, 1/345 and 1/147 so it "can sustain its own weight"). The proposed "detent-free leader" therefore already exists as a community configuration. (c) The damping-suppresses-tremor "twist" is established teleoperation and HRI knowledge (e.g., master-dynamics testbed arXiv 2107.10807; search snippets report that moderate damping and inertia improve target matching). The new part is linking it to imitation-learning labels. Taken together this is a known question in a new setting: 5.

## Feasibility (5.5):
(1) **The premise is unverified and physically odd.** I could not open issue #2074: the sandbox GitHub API returned 403 for huggingface/lerobot, and web search returned nothing on it. "~29 detents per output revolution" is about 12.4° per step. Motor cogging reflected through a 147–345:1 gearbox would give thousands of steps per output revolution, not 29. If 12° quantization were general, SO-101 teleoperation would be visibly unusable for fine tasks, and the community uses it for exactly those tasks. The week-1 histogram test is the right check, and I expect it to come back negative or specific to one unit. (2) **The fallback "resolution" factor is mostly an illusion.** The STS3215's 12-bit magnetic encoder reads the output shaft, so gear backlash changes how the leader feels, not what it reports. The leader's `get_action` reads `Present_Position` directly (lerobot `so_leader.py`). The AS5600 is also 12-bit, and uncalibrated off-axis magnets in printed parts can add nonlinearity of roughly a degree. "Stock = 4096 counts through backlash vs AS5600 direct" is therefore not a resolution contrast. (3) **The hardware is bigger than described.** On the SO-101 the servo bodies are structural, so a bearing-and-magnet leader means redesigning all six joints, not copying the CAD. A low-friction leader will also sag under gravity, which is exactly why the docs say the SO-101 uses gearing. The low-friction cell therefore also changes gravity load and operator fatigue unless springs or counterweights are added, and that is a confound. Matching the friction collar to a spring-scale "breakaway torque" matches mean Coulomb friction, not a position-dependent detent profile or viscous drag. (4) **The statistics are weaker than stated.** There is one ACT policy per condition, so the policy-level n is 1. The 25 rollouts measure rollout variance, not training variance, and seeds are checked only on the baseline. The 80 demos per condition are clustered within 2 operators and a few sessions, so treating them as independent (d ≈ 0.45) is pseudoreplication. The 2 operators are also the unblinded experimenters who know the hypothesis. ABCCBA cancels only linear practice effects, not asymmetric carryover (adapting to a sticky device). (5) The software side is genuinely easy. LeRobot's `Teleoperator` ABC has about 8 abstract methods, and `HomunculusArm` is a ready serial-sensor template. Breakouts on perfboard make the PCB optional, so data by Dec 20 is plausible if the CAD goes well. The budget (~$500) fits.

## Impact (5):
If detents reproduce, SO-101 users get a useful practical answer. If they do not, the result is a modest friction study whose most likely outcome is "ACT absorbs it." That outcome is useful as a null, but it lands as a workshop paper. The open encoder-leader PCB has limited reuse value: the SO-100 gearless leader, U-Arm (2509.02437, a ~$50 leader for LeRobot) and commercial encoder leaders (e.g., WowRobo OpenArm KER with TLE5012B) already fill that niche. The audience is LeRobot hobbyists and low-cost IL groups, so this fits an ICRA workshop rather than CASE/IROS main track.

## Lab appeal:
Winncy Du (SJSU) could plausibly mentor a sensor-characterization project, such as encoder linearity, friction measurement and a human-in-the-loop protocol. The imitation-learning half gets nothing from that lab. LEMUR fits only loosely (printable mechanics). The proposal itself admits neither lab does imitation learning, so expect help with measurement, not with the ML or the framing.

## Main criticism:
The core contrast is confounded, and possibly empty. The "detent" quantization rests on one unread issue and is physically implausible as stated. The sensor resolution is the same 12 bits in both arms. The new leader changes friction, gravity load, inertia and joint compliance all at once, yet the policy outcome has n = 1 per condition. The cleaner experiment needs no new PCB: an SO-101 leader against the same leader with gears removed (the SO-100 recipe) gives identical encoder, housing and kinematics with friction and detents removed.

## What would raise the score:
1. Run the detent histogram first, on 2+ leader units if possible, and read #2074 and its comments in a browser before committing. Drop the quantization framing if the clustering does not show up.
2. Replace the encoder-leader build with an SO-100-style gearless STS3215 leader as the low-friction arm. Add a stock leader with a software low-pass/EMA filter (LeRobot's HomunculusArm already uses an EMA) as a third arm. This isolates "label noise fixable in software" from "operator stick-slip." Keep the PCB as an optional release artifact.
3. Hold gravity load constant across arms (spring or counterweight), or measure and report it.
4. Make the policy the unit of analysis: at least 3 seeds per condition, plus a bootstrap over demo subsets, then analyze with a mixed model (operator and session as random or blocking factors) rather than a one-way ANOVA on pooled demos. Pre-register the primary metric (placement error) and the predicted direction.
5. Add 3–5 outside operators (classmates) for the demo-quality metrics, blind the rollout scorer to condition, and use a Latin square with practice blocks.
6. Measure the friction and detent torque profile against joint angle (a fishing scale at several angles), not just breakaway.

## Closest prior work found:
- LeRobot SO-100 leader (gears removed, encoder-only; https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/so100) vs SO-101 geared leader (https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/so101): the exact low-friction vs geared contrast, already deployed, never evaluated.
- ALOHA 2, arXiv 2405.02292: leader gripper friction cut about 10× (lower gear ratio, metal gears) plus gravity compensation; ergonomic motivation, no policy ablation.
- How to Train Your Robots?, arXiv 2503.07017: modality comparison (kinesthetic/VR/SpaceMouse) with downstream learning.
- Static Is Not Enough, arXiv 2601.13042: VR vs SpaceMouse user study (HRI'26 LBR).
- Teleoperator testbed for master–slave dynamics, arXiv 2107.10807: master damping and inertia effects on operator performance (snippet only).
- U-Arm, arXiv 2509.02437 (github.com/MINT-SJTU/LeRobot-Anything-U-Arm): ~$50 low-cost leaders for LeRobot; shrinks the novelty of the hardware artifact.
- GELLO, arXiv 2309.13037: passive joint-matched leaders, user study against VR and SpaceMouse.
- Understanding Behavior Cloning with Action Quantization (PMLR v306): theory says BC tolerates quantization under stability, which pushes toward Result B.

## Wrong links/claims in the proposal:
- "Stock STS3215 leader reads 4096 counts per revolution through gears with backlash": wrong. The encoder sits on the output shaft, so backlash does not enter the reading, and its resolution equals the AS5600's (12-bit). The "friction × resolution" fallback has no real resolution factor.
- The #2074 quote ("approximately 29 distinct positions per revolution", closed as not planned) could not be verified here (GitHub API 403, no search hits). This is unconfirmed, not shown to be false, but 29 steps per output revolution is hard to explain mechanically.
- Calling the "detent-free magnetic-encoder leader of identical kinematics" new: an equivalent already exists in the SO-100 gearless leader, and the proposal omits it.
- The driver template should be `HomunculusArm` (serial sensor, existing EMA), not `so_leader` (Feetech bus). This is minor.
- ALOHA 2 "improved leader ergonomics": correct (now verified, see above). Other cited arXiv IDs (2503.07017, 2601.13042) check out.
